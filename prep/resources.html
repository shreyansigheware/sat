Fewer resources, used properly, beats more resources.

Everything below has been checked and sorted by whether it actually moves a score in three weeks. The list is deliberately short: the failure mode at this stage is not lack of material, it is spreading thin across too much of it.

The constraint nobody names

The scarce resource is official practice tests, not study material

Free SAT material is effectively infinite. Full-length adaptive tests written by College Board are not — there are eight in Bluebook (tests 4–11; 1–3 were retired in 2025 as unrepresentatively easy). Every one spent on an unfocused or untimed session is gone, and third-party mocks do not predict a score the same way.

Ration rule for the run to 12 September

Roughly two tests a week, taken timed and in one sitting, each followed by a full error-log pass before the next. Daily volume comes from the Question Bank, not from burning mocks. Hold back the two newest tests for the final week — they best reflect current administrations, and they are the most honest predictor of the real sitting.

Tier 0 · non-negotiable

Official, free, and sufficient on their own to reach ~1400

If time is short, this section is the whole plan. Nothing below Tier 0 earns its place until these are being used properly.

Bluebook

College Board · desktop & tablet app

The actual testing application, and the only place to sit full-length adaptive practice under true conditions. Also where exam setup must be completed before test day — skipping that step can cost the sitting outright.

Core8 tests, ration them

SAT Suite Question Bank

College Board · web, filterable

Thousands of retired real questions, filterable by domain and difficulty. This is the daily drilling tool and the single largest stash of genuine College Board items anywhere. Most paid question banks are imitations of this one.

Coredaily volume

Khan Academy — Official SAT Practice

College Board partner · free course

Links directly to Bluebook results and generates targeted practice from them. Excellent for repairing identified content gaps. Widely reported as strong to ~1400 and not sufficient alone above that — the top band is decided by error discipline and pacing, which Khan does not teach.

Coregap repair only

Schoolhouse.world

Free peer tutoring · small groups

Free small-group SAT tutoring by certified tutors, founded by Sal Khan, available worldwide including India. The realistic use here is a booked slot on a specific weak domain rather than open-ended tutoring.

How to watch without wasting the hour

A video watched passively converts to nothing. Every one of the four above should end with something written down — a Desmos move to practise, a topic that felt shaky, a prompt to reuse.

If it did not produce a line in the error log or the study list, it did not happen.

Lateronly if bank runs dry

The one caution on AI-generated SAT questions

AI is reliable for explaining a question you already have and unreliable for writing new ones. Generated items drift from College Board's answer-choice construction, and practising against them trains the wrong instincts — the traps are the whole point of the test. Feed it real questions; never let it invent them.

Deliberately excluded

What was looked at and left out

Aggregator "best free tool" listicles

Most are affiliate pages. They rank by commission, not by score movement, and they all recycle the same four official resources with padding around them.

App-store SAT apps

Question quality is unverifiable and rarely matches College Board construction. The one exception worth naming is Daily Bite of Prep, which is just official Question Bank items repackaged for phone drilling — no signup, no ads.

Paid tutoring at this stage

Three weeks is too short for a tutor to learn the profile and change it. The same hours spent on error-log discipline against official tests convert better.

Anything promising a score in a fixed time

Including, in fairness, the framing of the NotebookLM video above. The workflow in it is sound; the "1500+ guaranteed, free" packaging is marketing. Take the method, discard the promise.
